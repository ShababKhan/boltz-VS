## 2025-06-30 - O(N²) Performance Bottleneck with Python list.index() in Tight Loops
**Learning:** Python's `list.index()` method operates with O(N) time complexity. When called inside high-frequency or inner loops over large datasets (such as parsing tens of thousands of molecular tokens or identifying atomic components during structure featurization), this leads to an insidious O(N²) performance degradation that severely bottlenecks the application.
**Action:** Always precompute static index lookups into dictionary mappings (`{item: i for i, item in enumerate(items)}`) prior to entering a loop to ensure O(1) time complexity for lookups, or precompute and store structural property index arrays globally (like `const.res_to_frame_atom_ids`) to eliminate redundant O(N) searches per token.
