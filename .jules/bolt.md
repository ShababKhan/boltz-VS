## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - [Replace list.index with dict lookup in featurizer]
**Learning:** Found an anti-pattern using `.index()` on lists inside tight token and atom processing loops (`compute_frames_nonpolymer`, symmetry mapping) in `featurizer.py`, `mol.py`, and `symmetry.py`. The `O(N)` list lookups scale poorly given the thousands of tokens/atoms.
**Action:** Replaced `.index()` with precomputed dictionary lookups (`res_to_frame_atom_ids`, `chain_asym_id_to_idx`, `mol_atom_names_ccd_to_idx`) to guarantee `O(1)` access time and avoid redundant computations inside the loops. When linting as Bolt, make sure to restrict `ruff` to the modified files to avoid large scope creep in diffs.
